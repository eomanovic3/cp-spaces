# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers provide technical leadership, enable team effectiveness, and align engineering execution with product priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Staff and support the engineering team, including coaching and performance development
- Provide technical direction and architectural guidance
- Remove engineering blockers and resolve cross-team dependencies
- Ensure quality, maintainability, and delivery consistency across the team

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: coach, unblock, and guide technical decision-making
- With Product Managers: align technical capacity and trade-offs with product priorities
- With Project Managers: coordinate timelines, risks, and execution constraints

---

## UX Researcher / Designer

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Designers ensure solutions meet user needs through research, prototyping, and design validation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and interaction design artifacts
- Validate design decisions based on user feedback and evidence
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria that reflect customer experience goals

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: refine user problems and feature priorities
- With Developers: translate designs into implementation guidance and review trade-offs
- With QA: support usability validation and test scenarios

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define test strategy, validate releases, and coordinate quality assurance across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans across unit, integration, regression, and smoke testing
- Drive automation and maintain coverage for key workflows
- Coordinate manual QA and release validation where needed
- Triage defects and help prioritize fixes with product and engineering teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: validate testability and support automation work
- With Project Managers: report quality status and release readiness
- With Product Managers: confirm that acceptance criteria are satisfied

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers provide the build, deployment, and operational foundation required for reliable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation
- Support deployment environments, configuration, and rollback procedures
- Ensure observability, logs, metrics, and monitoring are available
- Improve reliability and operational consistency across environments

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers: support build, deployment, and environment needs
- With QA: provide test environments and release validation hooks
- With Project Managers: support deployment windows and operational risk planning

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead ensures security requirements are integrated into the product and release process, and that risks are identified and mitigated early.

### Responsibilities
- Review systems, designs, and releases for security risks
- Define security standards, threat mitigations, and remediation priorities
- Support secure implementation and testing practices
- Coordinate with incident response when necessary

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: assess security implications of roadmap decisions
- With Developers and Engineering Managers: guide secure implementation and remediation
- With Project Managers: escalate security risks and track mitigation plans

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define metrics, analyze product impact, and help teams measure whether the work is delivering value.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and instrumentation requirements
- Build dashboards and evaluate product or operational data
- Validate that tracking and reporting are accurate and useful
- Translate results into decision-making insight for product and delivery teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: help define and assess achievement of success metrics
- With Developers: clarify instrumentation and technical data requirements
- With Project Managers: provide operating metrics and trend insights for planning

---

## Business Analyst / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements and help reduce ambiguity across business and delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements from stakeholders
- Clarify scope, acceptance criteria, and business priorities
- Translate process and business needs into actionable backlog items
- Validate solutions against business outcomes and stakeholder expectations

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: refine prioritization and requirements quality
- With Developers and QA: clarify business intent and expected outcomes
- With Project Managers: support alignment between stakeholders and team execution

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success teams surface real-world product issues, customer feedback, and operational context from production use.

### Responsibilities
- Triage customer-reported issues and summarize impact
- Provide reproduction details and operational context to the delivery team
- Share feedback on product usability and customer pain points
- Support communication during incident response and post-release follow-up

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Developers and QA: share reproduction steps and validate fixes
- With Product Managers: communicate customer needs and feature requests
- With Project Managers: inform urgency, stakeholder communication, and release risk
